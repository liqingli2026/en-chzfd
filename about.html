Our Story

Founded in [Year], Zofande New Energy is a leading high-tech enterprise specializing in the R&D, manufacturing and global distribution of electric vehicle charging equipment and smart energy solutions.

With a focus on safety, efficiency and interoperability, our comprehensive product portfolio includes CCS2 DC fast chargers, AC wallboxes, solar combiner boxes and high/low voltage switchgear, all designed to meet the evolving needs of commercial, public and residential charging infrastructure worldwide.

Backed by a robust in-house R&D team and rigorous quality control, we deliver reliable, future-proof solutions that comply with major international standards, including CE, IEC, and relevant regional certifications. Our commitment to innovation ensures seamless integration with diverse EV models and grid systems, enabling operators and end-users to deploy scalable, cost-effective charging networks with confidence.
