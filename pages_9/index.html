Company Profile

Zhongfande New Energy Group Co., Ltd., referred to as Zhongfande Group, was founded in 2019 and is committed to becoming a world-leading new energy electrical enterprise. In the fields of new energy vehicle charging piles and photovoltaic grid-connected cabinets, we provide innovative, efficient, and reliable solutions to promote the application and development of green energy. Zhongfande Group focuses on the R&D, production, and sales of new energy vehicle charging piles and photovoltaic grid-connected cabinets. Our products are widely used in residential, commercial, and industrial fields, providing customers with one-stop new energy solutions. Meanwhile, we also offer comprehensive services such as engineering design, installation, and maintenance to ensure customer convenience and satisfaction during use.
